About

I build and lead security programs that hold up in the real world — vulnerability management at enterprise scale, application security woven into how teams ship, and incident response that works under pressure.

I spent more than fifteen years at Georgia Tech defending a sprawling, open-by-design research network, eventually serving as Principal Information Security Engineer. Today I lead Vulnerability Management and Application Security at IHG Hotels & Resorts, securing systems that serve guests at thousands of properties around the world. I still teach the graduate security incident response course I co-wrote at Georgia Tech, because the best way to stay sharp is to explain it to smart people who ask hard questions.
